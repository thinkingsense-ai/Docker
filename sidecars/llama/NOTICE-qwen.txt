Local models used by the free-edition quick-start (each is downloaded when you build the sidecar images; none ships in this repository):

- Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, by Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team), the agentic-tier reasoning model. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
  https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507
  The quantised GGUF file is Unsloth's conversion (also Apache License 2.0): https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-GGUF
- SmolLM3-3B, by Hugging Face, the fast-tier model. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (GGUF conversion by Unsloth).
  https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B

Apache License 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 . Neither model carries a non-commercial restriction.
